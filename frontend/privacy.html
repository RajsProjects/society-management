Data Protection & Privacy Governance

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Samaaj (powered by CivicLink Technologies, "we," "us," or "our"). We provide an enterprise multi-tenant residential community management platform that connects Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs), Apartment Owners Associations (AOAs), property managers, and residents.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process, and protect personal and financial information when you access or use the Samaaj web application, mobile apps, or backend APIs ("Services").

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to fulfill residential community management functions:

A. Resident & Member Profile Information

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and optional profile photograph.
  • Apartment Association: Flat/unit number, block or tower, square footage, occupancy classification (Owner vs. Tenant), and society affiliation.
  • Authentication Credentials: Salted and peppered password hashes (we never store plain-text passwords).

B. Financial & Billing Records

  • Maintenance bill invoices, generation dates, due dates, breakdown of society dues.
  • Payment reference codes (UPI Transaction IDs, Razorpay transaction references, and timestamped receipts). We do not collect or store full credit card numbers or UPI PINs.

C. Community Engagement & Operations

  • Complaints & Grievances: Ticket titles, descriptions, categories (e.g., Plumbing, Security), status notes, and resolution logs.
  • Democratic Voting & Polls: Ballots cast on society resolutions. Ballots are cryptographically tied to verifiable resident units to prevent duplicate voting.
  • Announcements & Circulars: Broadcast alerts and read confirmations.

D. Society Registration Documents

  • Society registration certificates, deeds, and administrative proof documents uploaded to secure AWS S3 storage with presigned, access-controlled URLs.

3. Multi-Tenant Architectural Data Isolation

Our platform implements Strict Logical Multi-Tenancy at both the application and database layers:

  • All records are permanently partitioned with an immutable tenant key (societyId).
  • Security filters intercept every query to verify that residents, committee members, and admins cannot access or view data from any other residential community.
  • Cross-tenant queries are blocked with cryptographic tenant context validation.

4. Ownership Succession & Historical Record Preservation

In accordance with society governance regulations and financial audit laws:

  • When a flat undergoes an ownership or tenancy transfer, the previous resident’s profile details are archived into the flat's immutable Ownership History log.
  • Previous maintenance invoices, payment receipts, and historical complaints remain preserved for auditing and accounts reconciliation.
  • Access privileges for former owners are deactivated immediately upon transfer to ensure community privacy.

5. How We Use and Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or monetize resident personal data. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Authorized Society Administrators: Your elected management committee members for community governance and billing administration.
  • Essential Infrastructure Providers:
    • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud compute, S3 secure document vaults, and SES notification delivery.
    • Razorpay: Encrypted payment gateway processing for society maintenance dues.
    • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted multi-tenant database clusters.
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly required by applicable law, court order, or governmental law enforcement agencies.

6. Data Security & Storage

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of API and web traffic is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Data files, backups, and databases are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Authentication: Cryptographic JWT authentication with short-lived tokens and rate-limiting defenses against brute-force attacks.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and applicable data privacy frameworks, you have the right to:

  • Access and review your resident profile, bills, and submitted complaints.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate personal details through your society admin.
  • Opt out of non-critical marketing communications (statutory billing and emergency alerts cannot be opted out).
  • Request account closure and deactivation upon vacating the residential unit.
